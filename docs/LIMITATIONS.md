# Limitations and non-goals

Shown in the app's About page too. Keep wording plain.

## What the app does not claim
- **Terrain only (for now).** A match means slope, relief and roughness are similar at a stated scale. It says nothing about rocks, minerals, temperature, light, or chemistry unless those are shown separately.
- **Not a whole environment.** No place on Earth reproduces lunar or Martian gravity, vacuum, radiation or atmosphere.
- **Not safety or resource advice.** Similarity is not landing-site certification and not proof of water, ice or minerals.
- **"Not in checked records" ≠ new discovery.** It only means the place did not overlap the analog records we checked (listed with their dates). Literature review and expert assessment are needed before calling anything new.
- **Scale limits.** Mars comparisons use 200 m pixels and Moon comparisons 60 m, on a 10 km window. Small obstacles, boulders, and access roads are invisible at these scales.
- **Surface model.** The Earth elevation data includes trees and buildings; vegetated windows are flagged because they can look rougher than the ground really is.
- **Pilot coverage only.** Two Earth regions are searched, not the whole planet.
- **Score meaning.** The score is a configured similarity under versioned tolerances and weights, not a probability of scientific validity.

## Novelty labels
1. **Documented analog** — overlaps a site in the checked records.
2. **Not in checked records** — no overlap with the records checked on the stated date.
3. **Under review** — a person is checking literature or expert opinion.
